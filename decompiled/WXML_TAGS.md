# WXML 标签结构（从 page-frame.html 静态提取）

> 注：此小程序基于 Taro 框架编译，wxml 由动态模板（taro_tmpl + tmpl_0_X）生成。
> 完整 wxml 源码无法从编译产物 100% 还原，下面是各标签用到的属性清单。

## <button>
属性: `appParameter`, `bindagreeprivacyauthorization`, `bindchooseavatar`, `bindcontact`, `binderror`, `bindgetphonenumber`, `bindgetrealtimephonenumber`, `bindgetuserinfo`, `bindlaunchapp`, `bindlongpress`, `bindopensetting`, `bindtap`, `bindtouchcancel`, `bindtouchend`, `bindtouchmove`, `bindtouchstart`, `businessId`, `class`, `data-sid`, `disabled`, `formType`, `hoverClass`, `hoverStartTime`, `hoverStayTime`, `hoverStopPropagation`, `id`, `lang`, `loading`, `name`, `openType`, `plain`, `sendMessageImg`, `sendMessagePath`, `sendMessageTitle`, `sessionFrom`, `showMessageCard`, `size`, `style`, `type`

## <comp>
属性: `i`, `l`

## <image>
属性: `class`, `data-sid`, `id`, `lazyLoad`, `mode`, `showMenuByLongpress`, `src`, `style`, `webp`, `binderror`, `bindload`, `bindlongpress`, `bindtap`, `bindtouchcancel`, `bindtouchend`, `bindtouchmove`, `bindtouchstart`

## <scroll-view>
属性: `animation`, `bindanimationend`, `bindanimationiteration`, `bindanimationstart`, `binddragend`, `binddragging`, `binddragstart`, `bindlongpress`, `bindrefresherabort`, `bindrefresherpulling`, `bindrefresherrefresh`, `bindrefresherrestore`, `bindrefresherwillrefresh`, `bindscroll`, `bindscrollend`, `bindscrollstart`, `bindscrolltolower`, `bindscrolltoupper`, `bindtap`, `bindtouchcancel`, `bindtouchend`, `bindtouchmove`, `bindtouchstart`, `bindtransitionend`, `bounces`, `cacheExtent`, `class`, `data-sid`, `enableBackToTop`, `enableFlex`, `enhanced`, `eventPassive`, `fastDeceleration`, `id`, `lowerThreshold`, `pagingEnabled`, `refresherBackground`, `refresherDefaultStyle`, `refresherEnabled`, `refresherThreshold`, `refresherTriggered`, `reverse`, `scrollAnchoring`, `scrollIntoView`, `scrollIntoViewAlignment`, `scrollIntoViewWithinExtent`, `scrollLeft`, `scrollTop`, `scrollWithAnimation`, `scrollX`, `scrollY`, `showScrollbar`, `style`, `type`, `upperThreshold`

## <share-btn>
属性: `canShare`, `data-sid`, `id`

## <text>
属性: `class`, `data-sid`, `decode`, `id`, `selectable`, `space`, `style`, `userSelect`, `bindtap`

## <view>
属性: `animation`, `bindanimationend`, `bindanimationiteration`, `bindanimationstart`, `bindlongpress`, `bindtap`, `bindtouchcancel`, `bindtouchend`, `bindtouchstart`, `bindtransitionend`, `catchtouchmove`, `class`, `data-sid`, `hoverClass`, `hoverStartTime`, `hoverStayTime`, `hoverStopPropagation`, `id`, `style`, `bindtouchmove`

## <web-view>
